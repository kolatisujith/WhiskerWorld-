                    WHISKER WORLD — QUICK APPLICATION GUIDE

1. WHAT IS WHISKER WORLD?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Whisker World is a modern, ethical pet discovery and adoption web application
specialized in connecting loving families with newborn and young companion
animals (puppies, kittens, kits, baby birds, and young pets) from verified
stores, shelters, and licensed breeders.

Core Motto: "Every Paw Deserves a Loving Home."


2. WHO ARE THE USERS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ADOPTERS: Families or individuals searching for a pet. They can search, view
  certified health passports, locate stores on the live GPS map, bookmark
  favorites, submit adoption questionnaires, and track status.
- CAREGIVERS / PET OWNERS: Licensed stores, shelters, and ethical breeders who
  list pets, upload medical records, review adoption inquiries, and coordinate
  safe handovers.
- GUESTS: Anyone visiting the site can browse pets, watch the interactive demo
  video on Home, read How It Works, and check the About page.


3. KEY PAGES & WHAT THEY DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- HOME PAGE (/):
  * Hero search bar with quick breed/species lookup.
  * Interactive Cinema 4K Demo Video Player (simulates the entire platform tour).
  * Categories (Puppies, Kittens, Birds, Bunnies, Small & Furry).
  * Featured verified stores and featured young companion cards.
  * Direct deep-scroll support: appending `?scrollTo=demo` smoothly glides
    straight down to the video player.

- FIND PETS (/pets):
  * Comprehensive companion catalog with multi-parameter filtering:
    species, gender, age in weeks/months, size, fee range, and stores.
  * Real-time sorting (Newest, Oldest, Youngest, Price Low/High).

- PET DETAILS (/pets/:id):
  * Photo gallery, age in weeks and life stage (newborn, baby, young).
  * Certified Digital Veterinary Passport: DHPP, Rabies, deworming, microchip ID.
  * Direct "Apply to Adopt" button opening the adoption questionnaire.
  * "View on Map" button showing the exact nursery location.

- PET & STORE MAP (/map):
  * Interactive GPS mapping interface.
  * Pins representing verified pet stores and nurseries.
  * Distance calculations and driving radii (5, 10, 25, 50 miles).

- HOW IT WORKS (/how-it-works):
  * Interactive Role Switcher ("For Pet Adopters" vs "For Pet Stores").
  * 5-step detailed roadmap for each user journey.
  * Technical architecture breakdown (reactive state, geolocation, role guards).
  * Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) expandable cards.
  * Prominent "Tap Home to See Demo Video" buttons.

- ABOUT PAGE (/about):
  * Official mission and founding story tackling puppy mills.
  * 4 Core Guiding Values & platform impact metrics (1,250+ adoptions).
  * Leadership & Veterinary Advisory Board profiles.
  * The Whisker World 5-Point Ethical Guarantee.


4. THE ADOPTION WORKFLOW (HOW APPLICATIONS WORK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ADOPTER SUBMITS APPLICATION:
   - Adopter fills out the application form on Pet Details (reasons for adoption,
     pet experience, living environment, household pets, contact preference).
   - Application status becomes [PENDING].

2. OWNER / CAREGIVER RECEIVES & REVIEWS APPLICATION:
   - Owner receives inquiry on Owner Dashboard & Inquiries screen.
   - Owner opens "Full Application Form" (`ApplicationDetailsModal`) to review
     all applicant responses, living environment, experience, and contact data.

3. OWNER ACCEPTS APPLICATION & CONFIGURES HANDOVER & PAYMENT:
   - Owner taps "Approve" which opens the Handover & Payment Modal (`ApproveAdoptionDialog`).
   - Owner specifies:
     * Pet Delivery / Handover Method: Owner Home Delivery, Store/Shelter Pickup,
       Neutral Meetup (e.g. Vet Clinic), or Safe Pet Courier.
     * Handover Address: Pre-filled with store, clinic, or residence address.
     * Scheduled Date & Time: Handover schedule (e.g. "Saturday at 2:00 PM").
     * Delivery Instructions: Guidelines on pet carrier, leash, food, and records.
     * Payment Method: Cash on Handover, Direct Bank Wire, UPI / Mobile Wallet,
       Store Credit/Debit Card, or Fee Waived.
     * Adoption Fee Amount: Pre-filled with pet fee.
     * Payment Instructions: Bank account details, UPI ID, or cash instructions.
     * Personal Approval Note: Encouraging welcome message from owner.
   - When owner approves:
     * Status becomes [APPROVED]. Pet availability status becomes [PENDING] (Pending Adoption).
     * System automatically dispatches an official adoption email to the adopter's email address.
     * Email record is permanently archived in `email_notifications`.

4. ADOPTER RECEIVES EMAIL & VIEWS HANDOVER & PAYMENT DETAILS:
   - Adopter receives email notification with complete pet delivery and payment instructions.
   - Adopter Requests portal displays prominent "Handover & Payment Details" cards
     with one-tap "Copy Delivery Info" and "Copy Payment Details" buttons.
   - Adopter can open the interactive "View Received Email" dialog (`EmailPreviewDialog`)
     simulating an official email client anytime.

5. FINALIZING ADOPTION:
   - After in-person handover and payment completion, owner taps "Finalize Adoption".
   - Pet status permanently transitions to [ADOPTED].
   - All competing pending applications for that specific companion are automatically closed.


5. TECHNOLOGY STACK HIGHLIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Front-End: Flutter Web (Dart 3.5+)
- Database: Relational SQLite (sqflite_common_ffi_web via WebAssembly / native FFI, Schema V7)
- State Management: Provider (MultiProvider pattern)
- Routing: GoRouter with reactive authentication & role guards
- Design System: Material 3, Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans), Light & Dark mode
- Security: Salted HMAC-SHA256 password hashing (16-byte random salt)
- Notification Engine: In-app Email Notification System with full email previewer
- Testing: Comprehensive unit, widget, and integration test suites passing 100%
